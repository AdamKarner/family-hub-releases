Privacy & Data Transparency

Your household. Your information.

Family Hub organizes shared household information across compatible devices. This page explains the main technologies and permissions in the Android app, and how website feedback is handled.

Last reviewed: October 22, 2026

Household syncing

Family Hub uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to sign in and synchronize household data across devices. The app supports a built-in Firebase configuration or an imported Firebase project configuration. Which project stores synced data depends on the configuration selected on your device.

Synced information can include household schedules, lists, and app data you choose to share across your devices. Other devices signed into the same configured household may receive that data.

Important: Firebase access controls depend on the project's authentication and Firestore security rules. This page does not claim that every custom Firebase project has been audited or configured identically.

Information kept on your device

The app also stores device preferences locally, including display/profile settings and a device-specific Settings PIN if you enable one. Device-specific choices are distinct from household data that syncs.

If you import a Firebase project configuration, information needed to reconnect that project is saved by the app on the device. Treat configuration files and sign-in credentials as sensitive.

Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: used for syncing and internet-based features.
  • Approximate location: optional, for weather location functionality.
  • Calendar read access: used when the app opens a specific calendar event for editing; the permission is requested for that feature.
  • Install packages: supports Android app installation and update-related workflows. Android controls installation approval.

Android may present permission prompts as you use a feature. You can review and manage permissions in your device's system settings.

Feedback and support

Bug reports, feature requests, and support questions submitted on the Family Hub feedback page are collected using Notion Forms and managed in a private Notion database, rather than through the Family Hub Firebase sync system.

Reports may contain the description, device details, contact email, and screenshots you choose to provide. Avoid sending passwords, security codes, Firebase credentials, or private information about children or household members.

External services and links

Some app features connect to third-party services, including Firebase, online calendar sources, and weather sources. Links to release downloads, guides and Notion feedback may open in your browser, where the respective service's policies apply.

For details on Google's Firebase processing and Notion's handling of form responses, review their published privacy policies. This page is a technical transparency overview, not a representation that those providers cannot access data processed by their services.

Questions, corrections or deletion requests

Use Feedback & Support for privacy questions or to request help identifying which configured Firebase project holds your synced information. Please do not submit credentials or other sensitive details through the public form.

Scope: This page reflects a review of the application code and Android manifest. A full production privacy policy should additionally verify retention/deletion processes, third-party service behavior, and the effective Firestore rules for each deployment.
