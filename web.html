New family? Get Started with Google signup, then create one household for your family. Already connected? Sign in with your usual Google account. Your Google sign-in opens securely in the Kindlane web app.

Start without an Android device

Create your household, invite family members, set up Daily lists and rewards, add meals and grocery items, and add calendar events using your iPhone or computer. The household owner can assign children their Daily lists and permissions under Home → Settings → Household members on iPhone, or Household members in the desktop sidebar. To set up someone's own device, choose Devices there and create a setup code. A child does not need a Google account, and a grown up approves the device. See how setup codes work.

For Google Calendar, a grown up can open Calendar → More → Manage calendars and choose Connect Google Calendar. Kindlane asks Google for read only access, you tick the calendars to share, and everyone in the household sees them. They refresh by themselves for about an hour, then a grown up taps Refresh from Google. The web app never changes anything in Google Calendar. Read the calendar guide.

For Apple Calendar, Outlook or another calendar, export an .ics file and import it under Calendar. Imports contain a snapshot of the coming year, including recurring events, and do not subscribe to the calendar. Import an updated file to refresh them.

Calendar events you add in a browser sync between household web browsers. Calendars connected from Google, and events shared by Android, appear alongside them on every device.

Make it yours

On iPhone, open Home → Settings. On desktop, choose Preferences. Pick a style and accent color, choose Light or Dark, enlarge text, arrange Home cards, choose phone navigation and adjust calendar/weather preferences. Light is the default. Your preferences are saved for you in this browser.

Using Kindlane on iPhone

  1. Open Kindlane in Safari and complete sign-in in the app.
  2. To keep it handy, use Safari's Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen (wording may vary by iOS version).
  3. If asked to sign in again after opening it from your Home Screen, sign in there too. Safari and Home Screen app sessions may be separate.

On a computer, open the same web app in your preferred browser. Your permitted household data remains managed by the app, not by this marketing website.
