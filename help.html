Frequently asked questions

Search also filters these answers. Open a question for instructions and relevant links.

How do I create or join a household?

In current Family Hub builds, open Settings, then Household and Sync across devices. Sign in with your own Google account, and choose Create household or Join with a code supplied by the household owner. An existing household should not be recreated on each phone. Wall displays without Google can use an existing email account. Older families using a shared family login should follow the migration prompts before changing devices; the legacy Firebase setup guide is still available.

How do invites and kid permissions work?

The household owner chooses Invite a grown up or Invite a kid and shares a one-time code. After the person joins, the owner can assign a kid's Daily list and open Permissions. A kid's device shows only its assigned list, and giving or taking away points is not available on the kid's own device.

What happens to the meal plan on Monday?

Family Hub moves the plan on automatically each Monday: the finished week goes into Past weeks and a planned future week becomes This week. Later planned weeks are retained. In Meals, use the Plan menu for Week templates and Past weeks; Start a new week early is optional and should not be used just to test the feature with real household data.

How do I plan meals for more than one week?

Open Meals, This week. On a wide landscape tablet, multiple weeks can appear together. Swipe or tap the week arrows to move farther ahead, then tap an empty meal tile to fill it. Use Plan, then Week templates to save or apply a favorite meal week. Past weeks are available to review.

Where did the Calendar controls go on a tablet?

At the top of Calendar, choose Agenda, Week or Month. The plus button creates an event, and the menu contains Refresh, Manage calendars and Countdowns. The Month view takes up more space on a landscape tablet, with events for the selected day below the calendar.

How can I move a grocery item to the right aisle?

Open Grocery and use the item's aisle or move control to choose a different section. This reorganizes the shared list without editing the ingredient on a saved dish.

Can I give or take away points on the Daily list?

On a grown-up device, open the Daily list Today card and choose Give bonus or Take away. Enter the Settings PIN, select the person, amount and reason. Recent shows the change and provides Undo. The old separate Out of bed button has been removed. These controls are hidden on a kid's own device.

How do I change Family Hub's appearance?

Open Settings, This device, then Appearance & colors. Choose Classic, Modern or Matte, then Light or Dark and a supported accent. Your phone, tablet and wall display can each remember their own appearance. The public website's separate color picker does not change the app.

Can I replay the welcome tour or see what's new?

In the Android app, open Settings, then Help & Support. Use Replay the tour or What's new; Features and tips has quick demonstrations and options to show or hide tab tips. These are in the app, not an interactive tour on this website.

Which Family Hub APK should I use?

Choose the Android APK for phones and standard tablets. Choose the Wall APK for a compatible portrait wall display. For Hearth hardware, follow the Hearth installation instructions.

Android says the APK cannot be installed. What should I try?

Confirm Android is version 8.0 or newer, use the correct APK, and check whether the browser or Files app has permission to install from that source. Some managed devices or smart displays restrict external app installation. See the installation guide.

Do I need to enter an email to download?

No. The public download page does not currently require an email address or account. Open Downloads and choose the APK for your device.

Why is my second device not showing the same household?

In the newer household flow, sign in with your own Google account and join the existing household using the owner's code. If the family is moving from the shared login, finish and verify the owner's Move my data step before switching other devices. Families still using the older Firebase project must connect both devices to the same project and family account. Compare a known item on both devices before uploading or resetting anything. See legacy setup if needed.

Where can I find the newest version?

The Release Center lists published Android and Wall builds along with release notes. Updates should come from official Family Hub downloads.

How do I install on a Hearth display?

The dedicated Hearth walkthrough covers Advanced Settings, Developer options, wireless ADB, Bugjaeger, Native App, installation, and launching Family Hub.

Should I create a new household or connect to an existing one?

Only the first household owner creates a household. Everyone else joins with an invite code after signing in. If your family uses the older shared-login Firebase setup, continue using the same project until the data migration is completed and checked. The existing setup walkthrough explains that legacy path; do not create a second project by accident.

How can I tell whether syncing is really working?

First check that the app shows a successful connection rather than an offline or error state. Then compare a known item on two devices in the same household. A connection indicator alone does not prove both devices have the same information. Avoid uploading data from an empty device until you know which device has the correct records.

Why did a feature or theme disappear after updating?

Different APK builds may be created from different development branches. A numerically newer test build is not necessarily the newest version of every feature. Test APKs do not combine features automatically. Use the approved public Release Center for normal updates; if testing a private build, confirm its specific source branch and build notes before installing.

Will updating erase my information?

Compatible signed updates ordinarily preserve local app data, but that is not a substitute for verifying household syncing. Do not uninstall the app or clear its storage as a troubleshooting step unless you understand the impact. The Android and Wall variants can replace one another rather than install side by side.

Where is my household information stored?

Family Hub uses local device settings and Firebase-based household syncing. Support submissions go through a separate Notion form. The Privacy & Data page explains these categories and provides a route for questions or correction requests.

What should I include when reporting a problem?

Tell us what you expected, what happened instead, your device type and app version, and the steps to reproduce it. A screenshot can help, but don’t include passwords, Firebase configuration files, private account credentials, or sensitive household details. Submit a report here.
