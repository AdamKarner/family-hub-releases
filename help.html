How do I create or join a household?

On iPhone or computer, open Kindlane and choose Sign in with Google. Your first sign-in creates your account. New families enter a household name and choose Create household; invited family members enter their code and choose Join with a code. Returning members sign in with their usual Google account. No Android device or Firebase setup is required. Get Started with browser signup.

On Android, complete the welcome tour and choose Continue to Google, or open Settings → Sync across devices on an existing installation. Follow the household guide. Families using an older custom project can still use the legacy Firebase setup guide.

How do I rename my household?

Only the owner can. In the Android app, open Settings, then Household & sync, and tap Rename next to the household name. On the web, open Settings and change Household name, then tap Rename household. Other devices show the new name the next time they open Kindlane.

What if I cancel Google sign-in or choose the wrong account?

In the web app, try Sign in with Google again. If the wrong account is already signed in, use Home → Settings → Sign out on iPhone, or Preferences → Sign out on desktop, then choose the correct Google account. On Android, retry under Settings → Sync across devices. Canceling sign-in does not create a household. See the household setup guide.

If a code expired, request another from the owner. Keep your existing household when troubleshooting sign-in.

How do I preserve an older family login while moving to a household?

Existing shared-family accounts can still use Settings → Sync across devices → Using an older family login?. Keep that login and all devices intact until the owner signs in with their own Google account, creates the household, and uses Move my data on the device with the correct shared information. Verify your family data and second-device syncing before switching or removing anything. Do not uninstall or erase app storage to troubleshoot. The legacy Firebase guide remains available for existing installations.

How do I set up a phone, tablet or wall display with a setup code?

A grown up opens Household members → Devices on the web (on iPhone, Home → Settings → Household members), or Devices in the Household settings on Android. Pick the person, choose A child (no account needed) or An adult (signs in with Google), and choose Create setup code. The code has 6 characters, such as K7Q 4MX, works once and runs out after 14 minutes.

On the new device, choose Join with a setup code and type the code. On Android you can scan the QR code instead, except on a wall display. The new device shows a check code. A grown up sees the same check code under Devices and chooses Approve only if it matches. The device stays signed in afterward, so signing out needs a new code. The owner can choose Remove device at any time, and the person keeps their lists and points. If a code says it cannot be used, it may be old, already used or mistyped, so ask for a new one. Read the step-by-step guide.

How do invites and kid permissions work?

In the web app, open Home → Settings → Household members on iPhone, or Household members in the desktop sidebar. The owner creates an Adult or Child invitation and shares its 8-character code privately. Each code works once and expires after 24 hours. The recipient signs in with their own Google account and chooses Join with a code. On Android, the equivalent choices are Invite a grown up and Invite a kid under Members. To set up someone's own phone, tablet or wall display without a Google account, use a setup code under Devices instead.

After a child joins, the owner assigns their Daily list and reviews Permissions. Child controls limit available actions; shared household calendars and lists remain visible to household members.

My child doesn't have a Google account. Can I use a made-up email address?

A child does not need a Google account. A grown up can create a setup code for them under Household members → Devices, choosing A child (no account needed). A child can also use their own Google account with a Child invitation. The older email-and-password route is described next. Supported Android/Wall devices can sign a child in with an existing Firebase email-and-password account instead of Google, but typing a made-up address here does not create an account. The email-and-password user must first be created by someone authorized to manage Firebase Authentication for the project's household sign-in. An administrator may use a unique address as a login identifier, even if it has no inbox, but a fictional address cannot receive password-reset or verification messages. A working parent-controlled email address or unique alias is safer. On the child's device, choose No Google on this device? Sign in with email, sign in with the pre-created account, and then use the parent's Invite a kid code to join the household. Set the child's Daily list and Permissions on the owner's device. Never sign the child into the owner's account: their role and permissions follow the signed-in user. If you cannot create a separate email/password account for the active household, this is not currently a self-service sign-up path. Read the child account setup note.

What happens to the meal plan on Monday?

Kindlane advances the meal plan automatically each Monday. The finished week goes to Past weeks and the next planned week becomes This week, while later plans are kept. On a phone, tap the week heading to choose Past weeks; on tablets, use the week navigation. Week templates and Start a new week remain in the week actions menu. Starting a new week early changes household data, so do not use it just to test the feature.

How do I plan meals for more than one week?

In the web app, open Meals (Meal planner on desktop), choose a week and pick meals for its slots. Use Saved meals to add meals and dishes.

On Android, open Meals and choose This week. On an Android phone, tap the week heading and choose Plan ahead to create the next saved week, or choose an existing planned or past week. You can swipe between already-planned weeks. Tap an empty meal row to pick a meal. Auto-plan fills unlocked dinners in This week when Dinner is shown; Week templates, Start a new week and Delete this week are in the week actions menu as applicable. On a wide landscape tablet, multiple weeks can appear together; swipe or use week arrows to move ahead. Changes sync to your household when connected.

How do I connect Google Calendar to Kindlane?

A grown up opens Calendar → More → Manage calendars on the web, or Calendar → More → Manage calendars → Google Calendar on Android, and chooses Connect Google Calendar. Kindlane asks Google for read only access, you tick the calendars to share, and every household member sees them. Removing a calendar in Manage calendars deletes its copy from the household and does not change Google. On Android, a grown up can also turn on Edit inside Kindlane to add, change or delete events. Read the full calendar guide.

Where did the Calendar controls go on a tablet?

On an Android tablet, at the top of Calendar choose Agenda, Week or Month. The plus button creates an event, and the menu contains Refresh, Manage calendars and Countdowns. The Month view takes up more space on a landscape tablet, with events for the selected day below the calendar.

How can I move a grocery item to the right aisle?

Open Grocery and use the item's aisle or move control to choose a different section. This reorganizes the shared list without editing the ingredient on a saved dish.

Can I give or take away points on the Daily list?

On an Android grown-up device, open the Daily list Today card and choose Give bonus or Take away. Enter the Settings PIN, select the person, amount and reason. Recent shows the change and provides Undo. The old separate Out of bed button has been removed. These controls are hidden on a kid's own device.

How do I change Kindlane's appearance?

On iPhone, open Home → Settings. On a computer, choose Preferences in the sidebar. Choose Classic, Modern, Matte, Coral Home, Atmosphere or Opal; Light or Dark; an accent color; and Standard or Larger text. You can also arrange or hide Home cards, choose phone bottom-bar sections, select Home Daily lists and change calendar/weather preferences. New browsers start in Modern Light, and your choices are saved for you in this browser.

On Android, open Settings → This device → Appearance & colors. The public website's color picker changes only this website.

Can I replay the welcome tour or see what's new?

On iPhone, open Home → Settings and choose Welcome tour under Help. On a computer, choose Preferences in the sidebar, then Welcome tour under Household & account. The web app shows the tour once for each person in each browser, and you can skip it any time.

In the Android app, open Settings, then Help & Support. Use Replay the tour or What's new. Features and tips has quick demonstrations and options to show or hide tab tips.

The tour is in the app, not on this website.

How do I turn Pets or Upkeep on or off?

A grown up turns them on under Modules: Home → Settings on iPhone, Preferences on a computer, or Settings on Android. The switch applies to the whole household. Other grown ups can show or hide the tab on their own device. Kids' devices and wall displays never show Upkeep. Turning a module off hides it and deletes nothing. Pets cannot be turned off while a pet sitter still has access.

How do I add a pet and log feeding and walks?

Open Pets and choose Add pet. Type a name and pick a color. Each pet gets a card. Open the pet's page and, under Schedule, add a time for Feeding or Walks, with an amount like 1 cup and who does it if you like.

The card shows what is next, like Next 6:00 pm, or Due since once a time has passed. Tap Fed or Walked when it is done. Undo shows for a few seconds. My Day lists what is still due today.

How do I track pet medicine and vet visits?

On the pet's page, choose Add medicine. Type the name and dose and pick Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Every few days. Each medicine gets its own row on the card with a Given button.

Under Health, choose Add record for a vet visit, grooming, a vaccine, a weight or anything else. Set Next due, like 1 year. A visit today or later can have a time and shows on the Calendar. Coming up lists what is due in the next 30 days.

I forgot to log a feeding. Can I add it later?

Yes. Open the pet and scroll to Last 14 days, then choose Log earlier. Pick what, the day, the time and who did it. Tap an entry in the list to change or delete it. Kids and sitters can log up to a day back.

How do I give a pet sitter access?

The owner creates a setup code under Household members → Devices on the web, or Settings → Household & sync → Devices on Android, and picks Pet sitter. Type the sitter's name and their last day. A week is the default and 89 days is the most.

The sitter's phone joins with the code and shows only Pets, where they log Fed, Walked and Given and can see upcoming visits and records. Access ends after their last day. Any grown up can change the end date or end it early under Members.

Can Kindlane remind me about pet care?

On Android, open Pets and tap the bell. Turn on Feeding and walks, Medicine or Visits. Feeding, walks and medicine remind at their set times. Visits remind the evening before at 7:00 pm and an hour before. A time with a person set reminds only that person, and nothing reminds once it was logged. Each device chooses for itself. Reminders are not on the web yet.

How do I add Upkeep jobs?

Open Upkeep and choose Add job. Give it a name and how often, like every 3 months. The Starter list adds common house and car jobs in one go, spread out so the first weeks are not crowded. Add a car to give it its own card next to House.

Open a job to pick who does it, or leave it for anyone. Mine shows your jobs and Everyone shows them all. Pick several jobs at once to give them an owner or delete them together.

What happens when I mark an Upkeep job done?

Choose Done on the job and pick Today, Yesterday or Other day, with a note if you like. Undo shows for 10 seconds. The next due day counts from the day it was done. On the job's page, Snooze moves the due day by 2 weeks or 1 month.

How do Upkeep appointments, My Day and Ask someone work?

On a job's page, choose Add appointment. Pick the day, a time if you want one, and who is coming, like ABC Heating. The job then says Booked, and the appointment shows on the Calendar as Upkeep, and in Up next and My Day. Due days never go on the Calendar. Marking the job done clears its appointment.

Jobs you own show in My Day when they are due this week, with late ones at the top. Ask someone on a job's page opens Pass the Baton with the job's name filled in.

What are the ways to add a school email or flyer?

Kindlane keeps what school sends as a notice, then helps you turn it into dates, to dos, forms and things to bring. A grown up opens School and taps + Add at the top, which offers An item, Scan a flyer and Paste a notice. School has to be turned on first under Modules.

  1. An email. Share or paste its words.
  2. A newsletter page, like one on Smore. Kindlane splits it into its parts so you keep only what you need.
  3. A paper flyer. Take a photo of it.
  4. A screenshot, a picture or a PDF. Kindlane reads the words off it.

You always check the words before anything is saved. The questions below have every step for the Android app and for iPhone and computer.

How do I add a school email?

On an Android phone or tablet:

  1. Open the email in your email app, like Gmail.
  2. Press and hold a word in the email until it is picked. A small menu pops up. Tap Select all.
  3. In the same small menu, tap Share. If you do not see it, tap the three dots at the end of the menu first. Add to Kindlane in that menu does the same thing.
  4. Pick Kindlane from the list of apps.
  5. Kindlane asks Where does this go? Tap School notice.
  6. Take out anything you do not need, like a long signature, then tap Save.

On an iPhone or a computer:

  1. Open the email. Select its words and copy them. On an iPhone, press and hold a word, tap Select All, then Copy. On a computer, drag across the words and press Ctrl+C, or Cmd+C on a Mac.
  2. Open Kindlane, tap School, then + Add, then Paste a notice.
  3. Tap in the box and paste. Paste from clipboard does it for you where your browser allows it.
  4. Take out anything you do not need, then tap Save.

A notice holds about 4,000 characters, which is a long email. Paste only the part you need from a very long one.

How do I add a school newsletter, like one on Smore?

A newsletter is usually a web page, and the email only has a link to it. It is often far too long for one notice, so Kindlane shows its parts and you tick the ones to keep.

On an Android phone or tablet:

  1. Tap the link in the email so the newsletter opens.
  2. Tap the share icon at the top. It looks like three dots joined by two lines. In Chrome you can also tap the three dots, then Share.
  3. Pick Kindlane, then tap School flyer link.
  4. Wait while it says Reading the page.
  5. Pick what to keep opens. Tick the parts you want, like your school's dates. Small grey names with no checkbox are section titles.
  6. The line at the bottom shows how full the notice is. If it says Too long, untick a part.
  7. Tap Next, check the words, then tap Save.

On an iPhone or a computer: a browser is not allowed to read another site from a link, so you copy the page instead.

  1. Open the newsletter. Select all of it and copy. On a computer, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, or Cmd+A then Cmd+C on a Mac. On an iPhone, press and hold a word, tap Select All, then Copy.
  2. Open Kindlane, tap School, + Add, then Paste a notice.
  3. Tap in the box and paste. Pick what to keep opens by itself for a long page.
  4. Tick the parts you want, tap Next, check the words, then tap Save.

To keep more parts than fit in one notice, do it again and save the rest as a second notice.

How do I scan a paper flyer?
  1. Lay the flyer flat in good light.
  2. Open Kindlane, tap School, + Add, then Scan a flyer.
  3. On Android, tap Take photo. On an iPhone or a computer, tap Choose photos, which also offers the camera on a phone.
  4. Fit the whole page in the picture, hold still and take the photo.
  5. Kindlane says Reading the flyer, then shows the words under Check the scan.
  6. Fix any words it got wrong. Dates and times matter most. Then tap Save.

A flyer with two sides? Take both photos first, then choose up to 4 at once. A small copy of each photo is kept with the notice, for grown ups only. Open the notice and tap a photo to see it full size. The Android app reads better than the browser does, so use it for a hard to read flyer if you can.

How do I add a screenshot, a picture or a PDF?

On an Android phone or tablet:

  1. For a screenshot, press the power button and the volume down button together. Tap Share under it, then pick Kindlane.
  2. For a flyer longer than one screen, take a screenshot of each screen. Then open School, + Add, Scan a flyer, Choose photos, and pick up to 4.
  3. For a PDF attached to an email, open it, tap Share, then pick Kindlane. For a PDF saved on your phone or in Drive, tap Scan a flyer, then Choose a file.
  4. Fix any words it got wrong, then tap Save.

On an iPhone or a computer:

  1. Open School, + Add, then Scan a flyer.
  2. Tap Choose photos and pick up to 4 pictures or screenshots. Or copy a picture and tap Paste a picture.
  3. The first scan takes a little longer while the reader loads.
  4. Fix any words it got wrong, then tap Save.

PDFs are read on Android only for now. On an iPhone or a computer, take a screenshot of the PDF. A PDF with a lock on it cannot be opened anywhere.

How do I add a flyer from Seesaw or Canva?

These flyers only show once a browser runs the page, and the flyer is often one picture. Reading the link by itself finds nothing.

On an Android phone or tablet:

  1. Open the flyer, tap the share icon, pick Kindlane, then School flyer link.
  2. When it says no words were found, tap Open it here. The page opens inside Kindlane.
  3. Wait until you can see the flyer, then tap Read this page.
  4. Check the words, then tap Save.

On an iPhone or a computer:

  1. Open the flyer. Copy the picture, or take a screenshot of it.
  2. In Kindlane, open School, + Add, then Scan a flyer.
  3. Tap Paste a picture, or Choose photos for a screenshot.
  4. Check the words, then tap Save.

If nothing is found either way, a screenshot shared to Kindlane always works.

What do I do with a notice once it is saved?

The notice opens with its words at the top. From there:

  1. Tap Suggest items. Kindlane reads the notice on your device and offers dates, to dos, forms and things to bring. Tick the ones to add, or tap one to change it first, then tap Save selected. With more than one kid at school, pick a kid under For and every suggestion that names no kid is for them.
  2. Something already on School from another notice, within a week and still ahead, says Already on School and is not added twice. A No school day inside a break you already have counts too. When the school sent it again with a new day, it shows both days and Update the day, which moves the one you have. A No school day just before or after a break is its own day.
  3. Or tap + Add from this notice to add one yourself. The notice's words stay in view while you fill it in.
  4. Notices in the School menu lists every notice, newest first. Pasting the same one again opens the one you saved.
  5. Deleting a notice keeps the items you added from it.
How do I add something that happens every week?

Open School, tap + Add, then An item. Pick a Date or something to Bring, then Every week, and tick the days, like cello on Tuesdays and Fridays. Add an end day if it stops. It shows under each of its days in This week.

How do I clear out old school items or start a new school year?

Clear older: on the School tab, tap Show on Done. When something was done more than 60 days ago, Clear older shows under it. It says how many items go, for every kid, and asks first. Notices stay.

New school year: tap the three dots at the top of School, then New school year. Each kid moves up a grade, like 3rd grade to 4th grade, and the teacher starts empty so you can type the new one. A grade Kindlane can't read is left for you. Clear done items deletes everything done or past, this year's too, and notices older than 30 days. Open to dos and forms, anything still ahead, and the notices they came from stay. Untick it to keep them.

From June to August the School tab asks once: A new school year? Once one grown up moves the grades up, other devices don't move them again that year. Grown ups only.

How do I pause weekly things during a school break?

Add the break as a No school date. Open School, tap + Add, then No school day or break. Every kid at school is ticked. Pick the first day and, for a break, the last day, then tap Add. Suggest items also offers one when a notice says No school, Closed or No classes.

On those days, those kids' weekly things, like cello on Tuesdays, are hidden on the School tab, Home, the widget, the Wall, reminders and the Calendar. They come back after the break. Something for two kids stays on the shared views unless both are off, but on one kid's own device, chip and Wall card it is hidden on their days off. One time things on those days still show and still remind. To change a break, open it and tap Edit.

Why is my newsletter too long for one notice?

A notice holds about 4,000 characters so it stays quick to read. A district newsletter can be ten times that. On Pick what to keep, tick only the parts you need, like your own school's dates. To keep more, add the page again and save the rest as a second notice.

Words pasted or shared into Paste a notice, and a long scan, show a count near the end, like 3,950 of 4,000 characters. When the end did not fit, the box says so. Tap Copy the rest before you tap Save, then paste it as a second notice. Trimming the top or fixing a word keeps the rest. Pasting a different notice over the box drops it.

Why were no words found, or why does a part say No words here?

The words are inside a picture, like a lunch menu or a calendar image, or the page only shows once a browser runs it. On Android, tap Open it here, wait for the flyer to show, then tap Read this page. Anywhere, you can take a screenshot of that part and scan it. If a scan finds no words, try again in good light with the whole page in the picture.

Does a school flyer or email leave my device?

Photos, screenshots and PDFs are read on your own device or in your own browser, not by an outside service. For a link on Android, your device opens the page itself, once, the way a browser does. The words you save become a notice, and a small copy of each photo or page is kept with it. Both sync to the grown ups in your household like the rest of Kindlane. Kids and sitters cannot see notices or their pictures.

Can Kindlane read my school email by itself?

No. Kindlane never looks in your inbox. You choose each email or flyer and add it. That keeps the rest of your mail private.

Why did Suggest items miss something?

It reads a sentence at a time and only offers what it can tell. It needs a school word like due, bring, field trip or rehearsal, and a day. A trip named by where it goes, like a zoo trip or a class trip to the museum, counts when it has a day still ahead, and a lunch to pack after it goes on the trip day. Something that repeats, like a rehearsal every Tuesday, is offered every week, but an end day is never read from the notice. Add one with Edit if it stops. A day named with no date, like Monday on a spirit week flyer, means the coming one, so scan a flyer before its week starts, or check the days. Check the notice yourself and use + Add from this notice for anything it left out.

How do I ask the other grown up to sign a form?

Open the form or to do on School and tap Ask someone. Pick who, then send. It becomes theirs, and the item says Asked with their name and the day.

When they mark the request Done, a to do is done and a form is returned by itself, so nobody ticks it twice. If they say no, or you take the ask back, the item says so and goes back to whoever had it before, even Anyone. Ticked by hand first? Not done yet still opens it again. Asking a kid keeps Who does it as it was. Picking someone else under Who does it ends the ask.

How do I get reminders for school?

On Android, the School tab asks once: Get a reminder for forms and to dos named on you? Tap Turn on and allow notifications. They come the evening before at 7:00 pm and the morning of at 7:00 am. The bell at the top of School has Things to bring and School dates too. Each phone and tablet picks its own. A break reminds once, before it starts. If you tapped Don't allow, the School card says notifications are off and opens Android's settings to turn them on. The web app does not remind.

Why does School say a change could not be saved?

When the household does not allow that change, like a kid ticking something their switch no longer covers, School says to ask the household's owner. Offline, changes wait and save when you are back online. On the web app, the School tab says when it is offline.

Who can see a school notice?

Only grown ups in the household, and the same for a flyer's pictures. The dates, to dos, forms and things to bring you add from it show to everyone, the same as other School items. A notice never shows on a kid's device or a wall display.

How do I ask someone to do something with Pass the Baton?

On Home, open Pass the Baton and tap Ask. Choose a member, describe the request and optionally add a time. The recipient can tap Seen it, Accept or Decline, and Mark done. Acceptance can be undone, and the sender can cancel. Requests require household syncing and are intended to be visible only to the sender and recipient.

How do Pass the Baton notifications work?

In the Android app, open Settings, This device, then Pass the Baton notifications. Alerts are off until you turn them on. Android notification permission and battery restrictions can affect delivery, so test alerts between real household devices before depending on them.

Can I use Kindlane on an iPhone or computer?

Yes. Open Kindlane in Safari on iPhone or in your computer browser. Sign in with Google to create your account, create a household or join with an invite. You can set up Daily lists, rewards, meals, groceries and calendar events without an Android device. On iPhone, Safari's Share menu offers Add to Home Screen. Read the iPhone and Computer guide or Get Started.

Which Kindlane APK should I use?

Choose the Android APK for phones and standard tablets. Choose the Wall APK for a compatible portrait wall display. For Hearth hardware, follow the Hearth installation instructions.

Android says the APK cannot be installed. What should I try?

Confirm Android is version 8.0 or newer, use the correct APK, and check whether the browser or Files app has permission to install from that source. Some managed devices or smart displays restrict external app installation. See the installation guide.

Do I need to enter an email to download?

No. The public download page does not currently require an email address or account. Open Downloads and choose the APK for your device.

Why is my second device not showing the same household?

On the web, sign in with the Google account already linked to your household. A new family member signs in with their own account and joins using the owner's code. Check that the intended Google account is selected and compare a known item on both devices. Calendars a grown up connects with Connect Google Calendar appear on both the web and Android. Browser-created calendar events sync between web browsers.

For an older Android shared-login setup, verify the owner's Move my data step before switching other devices. Families still using a custom Firebase project should follow legacy setup and preserve their existing information.

Where can I find the newest version?

The Release Center lists published Android and Wall builds along with release notes. Updates should come from official Kindlane downloads.

How do I install on a Hearth display?

The dedicated Hearth walkthrough covers Advanced Settings, Developer options, wireless ADB, Bugjaeger, Native App, installation, and launching Kindlane.

Can I install Kindlane on a Skylight Calendar Max?

The Skylight Calendar Max guide explains the community-reported USB ADB route and optional wireless pairing. Updated firmware can block the original unlock method, and the portrait Wall layout has not been validated on Skylight hardware. Check the warning before making any device changes.

Should I create a new household or connect to an existing one?

Only the first household owner creates a household. Everyone else joins with an invite code after signing in. If your family uses the older shared-login Firebase setup, continue using the same project until the data migration is completed and checked. The existing setup walkthrough explains that legacy path; do not create a second project by accident.

How can I tell whether syncing is really working?

First check that the app shows a successful connection rather than an offline or error state. Then compare a known item on two devices in the same household. A connection indicator alone does not prove both devices have the same information. Avoid uploading data from an empty device until you know which device has the correct records.

Why did a feature or theme disappear after updating?

Different APK builds may be created from different development branches. A numerically newer test build is not necessarily the newest version of every feature. Test APKs do not combine features automatically. Use the approved public Release Center for normal updates; if testing a private build, confirm its specific source branch and build notes before installing.

Will updating erase my information?

Compatible signed updates ordinarily preserve local app data, but that is not a substitute for verifying household syncing. Do not uninstall the app or clear its storage as a troubleshooting step unless you understand the impact. The Android and Wall variants can replace one another rather than install side by side.

Where is my household information stored?

Kindlane uses local device settings and Firebase-based household syncing. Support submissions go through a separate Notion form. The Privacy & Data page explains these categories and provides a route for questions or correction requests.

What should I include when reporting a problem?

Tell us what you expected, what happened instead, your device type and app version, and the steps to reproduce it. A screenshot can help, but don’t include passwords, Firebase configuration files, private account credentials, or sensitive household details. Submit a report here.
