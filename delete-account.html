Account deletion

Delete your Kindlane account

You can delete your account and its data from inside Kindlane, on Android or on the web. It takes a minute and can't be undone.

Last reviewed: November 18, 2026

In the app

  • Android: open Settings, then Household & sync. At the bottom, tap Delete my account.
  • Web: open Settings, then Household & account. Under Sign out, choose Delete my account.
  • Type DELETE to confirm. If you signed in with Google a while ago, Google asks you to pick your account again first.
  • When it's done, Kindlane starts fresh on that device.

A pet sitter finds Delete my account at the bottom of their pets screen. On a kid's device, the button is under Parents Only.

What is deleted

  • A family member, kid or pet sitter: their sign-in, their place in the household, their private My board notes and their finished Pass the Baton requests.
  • The household owner: the whole household, for everyone. That means every list, note, calendar, meal, pet, photo, points history and device record, plus every member, then the owner's sign-in.

What is kept

  • When a family member deletes only their own account, the things they added for everyone stay with the family. That includes grocery items, family notes, calendar events and pet care entries.
  • When the owner deletes the household, other members' private My board notes and Pass the Baton requests can't be read by anyone afterwards, since only their owner could ever read them. Each member can delete their own account first if they want those removed too.
  • Feedback you sent through the support form is kept separately, in Notion. Ask there to have it removed.

Can't open the app?

Ask through Feedback & Support. Say it is a deletion request, and which Google account or household to delete. We delete it by hand and reply when it's done. Please don't send passwords or setup codes.
