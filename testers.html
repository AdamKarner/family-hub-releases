Google Play testing

Become a Kindlane tester.

Try new versions of Kindlane on Android before everyone else, straight from the Play Store, and help us find what needs fixing.

  • Android phones and tablets
  • Android 8 or newer
  • A Google account

  1. Join the testers group

    Use the same Google account that your phone's Play Store uses. Joining is free and you can leave any time.

    Join the testers group

  2. Accept the test in Google Play

    Give it a few minutes after joining, then open this page and tap Become a tester.

    Accept the test

  3. Install from Google Play

    Kindlane shows up in the Play Store for your account. Updates arrive on their own, like any other app.

    Open Kindlane in Google Play

Already using the Kindlane APK from this website?

Uninstall it before you install from Google Play. Google signs Play versions with a different key, so they can't install over the website version.

Your household is saved online and comes back when you sign in. Settings kept only on that device, like your style and Home layout, start fresh.

Wall displays

Keep wall displays on the Wall APK. The Play test is for phones and tablets.

Found a problem?

Tell us on the feedback page. You can leave the test any time from the Accept the test page.
