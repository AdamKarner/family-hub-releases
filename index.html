Get started

Getting started is simple.

01

Download Family Hub

Choose how your family uses Family Hub: iPhone or Computer in a browser, or Android and Wall display with their approved APKs.

02

Open or install

On iPhone or Computer, open Family Hub in your browser—there is no APK to install. On Android or a Wall display, use the appropriate APK and follow your device's installation instructions.

03

Make it yours

If you're already part of a household, sign in inside the app. For invitations or older family setups, follow the household setup guide. For browser access, see the iPhone and Computer guide.
