Install Family Hub on Hearth.

A step-by-step walkthrough for replacing the default Hearth interface with Family Hub using an Android phone and Bugjaeger.

HEARTH-SPECIFIC WALKTHROUGH

Install Family Hub on a Hearth display

This walkthrough follows the installation process tested on a Hearth display. You’ll use an Android phone and the Bugjaeger app to install Family Hub wirelessly. These steps are specific to Hearth; other Android displays may use a simpler installation method.

1

Open Hearth Advanced Settings

On your Hearth display, tap the Settings icon in the upper-right corner. Then select the small Advanced Settings button in the lower-left corner.

2

Enable Developer Options

In Advanced Settings, open About tablet. Tap Build number seven times to unlock Developer options, then open Developer options.

3

Enable wireless ADB

Under the Debugging section, open Wireless debugging and enable it. Make a note of the IP address and port displayed in the Hearth settings—you’ll need both to connect your phone.

4

Connect Bugjaeger from your phone

On your Android phone, install Bugjaeger from Google Play and open it. Enter the Hearth display’s IP address and port, then connect. Both devices must be able to communicate over your network.

5

Disable Native App

In Bugjaeger, scroll to the bottom of the app list and locate Native App. Disable it so the original Hearth interface does not launch in place of Family Hub. This changes the device’s default behavior.

6

Install Family Hub Wall

Download the Family Hub Wall APK to your phone. In Bugjaeger, tap the + button, find the APK in your phone’s Downloads folder, and install it on the Hearth display.

7

Launch Family Hub

Once installation finishes, look for Family Hub near the top of Bugjaeger’s app list. Tap the Play button to launch it on Hearth.

After installation: Turn off Wireless debugging in Hearth’s Developer options when you’re finished. If you want to return to the original Hearth interface later, you may need to re-enable Native App.
