Features

Everything your family runs on.

One home for the calendar, routines, meals, groceries and the little jobs in between. Here is what each part does.

Home

Your day at a glance.

Open Kindlane and the important things are already there.

  • What is up next, and what is for dinner tonight
  • Countdowns to the days everyone is waiting for
  • The next few days of the family calendar
  • My Day: one person's events, tasks and requests in one card

Phone, tablet, Wall and web

Calendar

Every family calendar in one place.

Everyone's schedule, merged and color coded, so nothing sneaks up on you.

  • Agenda, Week and Month views
  • Connect Google Calendar, or add calendars by link
  • Add and edit events in the app
  • Countdowns to trips, birthdays and holidays

Phone, tablet, Wall and web

Daily lists

Routines kids can actually follow.

Each kid gets their own list, split into parts of the day, with points to earn.

  • Morning, After school, Evening and Bedtime
  • Points, streaks and rewards to save for
  • Grown ups can give a bonus or take points away
  • Kids only see their own list

Phone, tablet, Wall and web

Meals

Dinner, planned.

Pick a meal for each day and the week takes care of itself.

  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner for every day
  • Plan weeks ahead and save favorite weeks
  • Saved meals with their ingredients
  • Monday brings the next plan forward on its own

Phone, tablet and web

Grocery

A list that writes itself.

Ingredients from the week's meals land on the list, sorted by aisle.

  • This week, One time and Always buy lists
  • Items grouped by aisle, and you can move them
  • Check things off together in the store
  • Share the list as text

Phone, tablet and web

Pass the Baton

Ask without the nagging.

Hand a job to someone else in the family and see when it is handled.

  • Send a request with a note and a due time
  • They can accept, decline or mark it done
  • Requests stay between the two of you
  • Kids can send and get requests too

Phone, tablet and web

Sticky notes

A corkboard for the whole family.

Leave a quick note for yourself or for everyone.

  • My board for private notes
  • Family board everyone can see
  • Notes match your colors and style
  • Starting on Android, coming to the web
